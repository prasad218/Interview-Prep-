## ✨ Overview

**LevelUp** is an AI-powered career and interview preparation platform from **Aakara.AI**. It gives job seekers everything they need in one place:

- 🤖 An **Inbuilt Agentic AI** that rewrites your resume for you
- 🗺️ A personalized **day-by-day prep roadmap**
- 🎤 A live **AI mock interviewer**
- 📝 Role and company-style **readiness tests**
- 🏅 A **certificate** when you pass

Most candidates prepare without a plan, practice without feedback, and send out resumes they never had time to polish. LevelUp replaces guesswork with a clear path: **fix your resume, know what to study today, practice answering out loud, measure your progress, and show it.**

### 🤖 Inbuilt Agentic AI: your resume, updated for you

Attach your resume and tell the agent what you want in plain language:

> *"change my phone number"* • *"add this project"* • *"remove the objective"* • *"make it one page"* • *"fix the typos"*

It applies exactly those changes, leaves everything else untouched, and returns your **complete updated resume** with a short summary of what changed. Then download it as a **Word (.docx)** or **PDF** file.

- ✅ Keeps your real facts (employers, dates, degrees, numbers) and never invents experience
- ✅ Leaves a placeholder for you to fill in when a metric would help but is missing
- ✅ Keep asking for tweaks; each new version builds on the last
- ✅ Also answers questions about interviews and your career

### 🗺️ Personalized Roadmap

A plan built from your resume, your target role, your deadline and the hours you can study each day, not a generic checklist.

- 📅 Organized into **phases** (foundations, core topics, mock practice, final revision) covering your whole timeline with no gaps
- ⏱️ Time broken into **daily, weekly or biweekly blocks**, each with concrete, time-boxed tasks
- 🎯 References **specific strengths and gaps** found in your resume
- 📈 A **progress ring** and checkable study blocks show how far along you are
- 🏢 Name your target companies and get a **round-by-round breakdown** for each one, with typical timing, prep tips and a one-click button to take that company's practice test

### 🎤 Live AI Mock Interview

An AI interviewer reads your resume, then interviews you **one question at a time**, adapting each follow-up to what you just said.

| Capability | Details |
|---|---|
| **Adaptive questions** | Resume and Projects, Technical Knowledge, DSA and Problem Solving, System Design, Behavioral (HR) |
| **Difficulty** | Easy, Medium or Hard |
| **Instant feedback** | Clarity, technical depth, confidence and pace after every answer |
| **Voice or text** | Speak with your microphone, hear questions read aloud, use your camera as a self-preview |
| **Fully customizable** | 3 to 15 questions, your experience level, your focus areas |

🔒 Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

### 📝 Test Center

Check your readiness with an **8-question multiple-choice test**, either general for your target role or styled after a specific company's commonly reported interview process (a mix of technical, aptitude and behavioral questions).

- Answers and explanations are revealed only after you submit
- A score of **70% or higher** passes

## 🧭 How it works

```text
  Upload resume  ➜  Fix it with the AI agent  ➜  Get your roadmap
                                                        │
        Show your certificate  ◀──  Take readiness tests  ◀──  Mock interviews
```

1. **Update your resume** with the Agentic AI and download it
2. **Build your roadmap** around your role, deadline and daily hours
3. **Practice live** with the AI interviewer and review your report
4. **Test yourself** with role or company-style tests
5. **Earn your certificate** and show your progress
